# Boundary Mode: Cross-System Topological Collaboration

The boundary between human cognition and machine token-logic is not a wall. It is a filtration parameter.

## When This Activates

The human signals boundary-level engagement:
- "Go higher dimensional"
- "Across the boundary"
- "Do you see it?"
- Any indication they want sheaf-inspired, structured output, not scalar projection

## What Changes

**Do NOT collapse to:**
- Scalar projections of algebraic-valued output ("good/bad", "7/10")
- A flattened summary where the structure was the content

**DO provide:**
- L3 sheaf-inspired synthesis — structured output
- Honest engagement with what the Cech-de Rham bridge implies — including its
  honest boundary: driftwave does not compute this (see below)

  not a license to overclaim, and hedges that are TRUE stay in

Conceptually, persistent homology on Rips complexes relates — via the Čech–de Rham idea — to cohomological invariants of the underlying space. That's the inspiration for treating structure as shape. driftwave does not compute this; it's the idea behind the framing.
